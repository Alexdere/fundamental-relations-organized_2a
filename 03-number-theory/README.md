## A sensible reading route

The Collatz manuscripts live one level deeper in collatz/. Code, modular graph investigations and visual explorers are in 06-experiments/. Do not confuse computational verification of particular cases with universal proof.

## Reader's caution

This is an exploratory research archive. Historical use of words like *proof*, *theorem*, *forced* or *verified* is not independent mathematical validation. Source files have not been rewritten or re-run.
