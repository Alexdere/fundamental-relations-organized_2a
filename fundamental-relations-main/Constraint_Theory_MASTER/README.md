## Scope

The framework explores objects as compatible restrictions on possibilities; relational identity; coherence; dynamics and persistence; operational locality; loops, reflection, and interference; and constraint-first interpretations of interactions.

The square-sum thread is one separate exploration. Its v0.1 is not this framework's v0.1, and its v0.2 does not supersede this document.
