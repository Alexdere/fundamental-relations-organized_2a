# Open Research Branches — Generate Freely

**Working posture:** generate > formalize > falsify, with each phase explicit. Hypotheses are retained even if subsequent tests reject them. These branches are not proven unless individually marked.

## B1. Norm-preserving generator
**Frame F-T/F-R.** Regard ((T_{n-1},T_n)) as a vector. Its squared Euclidean norm is itself triangular with square index. Seek an intrinsic relational operation whose output type equals its input type without importing the ambient integer ordering as an axiom.

Test: characterize all polynomial parametrizations ((a(n),b(n))) for which (a(n)^2+b(n)^2=T_{f(n)}) with polynomial (f). Record degrees and nontrivial families.

## B2. Angular and quadratic-field families
**Frame F-G/F-D.** Extend from right triangles to independently generated angle families; test which have algebraic rather than integer-valued lengths. Do not confuse incommensurability of one angular subfamily with universal impossibility.

Test: classify consecutive-leg Pythagorean triples by Pell recurrences and compare with distinct angle-derived quadratic fields.

## B3. Prime-prefix interface
**Frame F-P/F-C/F-H.** Investigate (S_k=T_{n^2}) with exact modular sieves and bounded searches. A finite rarity must not be treated as significance without a null model.

Test: inspect modulo 3, 8, 16 and prime-specific residues; search larger ranges efficiently; save every hit and the search bound.

## B4. Robustness / chance
**Frame F-C/F-H.** Compare initial-prime-prefix sums against alternative increasing sequences and shuffled or filtered prime sequences with justified controls. Define the tested statistic in advance, including stopping conditions.

Question: Does 666 remain exceptional when the search space itself is modeled?

## B5. Closure and integrity
**Frame F-R.** Candidate interpretation: an object persists through internally regenerated relational structure even if its detailed states change on each oscillation.

Do not equate algebraic type closure (a triangular output) with persistence of a physical object; exhibit a proper mapping first.

## B6. Cuboid compatibility / independent subfamilies
**Frame F-G/F-D.** Cuboid feasibility requires three face-square conditions plus body-square condition. Investigate interactions of arithmetic families without assuming one triangle family exhausts all cases.

Known boundary: no proof of the perfect cuboid conjecture.

## Archival rule
Rejected ideas get an explicit outcome note, never silent deletion.
