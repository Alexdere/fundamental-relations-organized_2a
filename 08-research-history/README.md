# Research chronology and provenance

May 16–22 session notes, ongoing syntheses, finding logs and conversation summaries, including disagreements and revisions.

## A sensible reading route

Read chronologically before concluding a statement was the final position. Multiple summaries can have non-overlapping insights. The two May 19 cascade-coordinate addenda are identical byte-for-byte.

## Reader's caution

This is an exploratory research archive. Historical use of words like *proof*, *theorem*, *forced* or *verified* is not independent mathematical validation. Source files have not been rewritten or re-run.
