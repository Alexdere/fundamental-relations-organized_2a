# When abstract relations meet physics

Essays and documents about physical interpretation, motors/propulsion, geometry and the possible role of impedance and flow.

## A sensible reading route

Begin with pumps_essay.md; the corresponding PDF is a separate export. The_Colour_of_Geometry_Constraint_Before (1).pdf is a standalone manuscript. Interpretations and proposed machines are unvalidated unless separately tested.

## Reader's caution

This is an exploratory research archive. Historical use of words like *proof*, *theorem*, *forced* or *verified* is not independent mathematical validation. Source files have not been rewritten or re-run.
