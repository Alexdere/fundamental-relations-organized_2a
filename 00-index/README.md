# Navigation, provenance and claim status

The master navigation documents: complete original-to-new filename correspondence, exact-duplicate record, and conventions for reviewing the mathematical claims.

## A sensible reading route

Begin with FILE_MAP.csv to locate files, then CLAIM_STATUS.md to understand the review categories. DUPLICATES.md lists byte-identical copies, not merely similar ideas.

## Reader's caution

This is an exploratory research archive. Historical use of words like *proof*, *theorem*, *forced* or *verified* is not independent mathematical validation. Source files have not been rewritten or re-run.
